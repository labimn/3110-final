Design and decisions
Savings.goal is abstract so callers cannot directly overwrite saved totals.
The module uses immutable lists and returns result errors for invalid operations.
A list belongs to one user; the caller must select the correct user's list.
Savings reuses Transaction.parse_text and parse_date; DOM handling stays in bin/.
Whole cents are represented internally by exact integer-valued floats, bounded
at 9,000,000,000,000,000 cents for native/JavaScript arithmetic. Targets and total
saved are capped at $90 trillion. Fractional cents are rejected (with a small
conversion tolerance for binary floating-point representation).
Past deadlines are permitted. Deadlines do not prevent later contributions.
Overfunding is permitted. Saved retains all contributions; remaining is at least
zero and progress is capped at 100%. Lowering a target preserves saved history.
Contributions are separate allocations and do not create income/expense records.
Automatic balance deductions require an explicit team decision and integration.

Validation completed
Commands: dune build; dune test.
Results: build passed; existing transaction/budget suite passed; 57 new OUnit
checks passed. Test expectations use hand-derived examples, not implementation
outputs: $250 toward $1,000 means $750 remaining and 25%; $250 toward $200
means zero remaining and 100%; 10 cents + 70 cents reaches an 80-cent target.
Parser checks cover whitespace, empty fields, zero/negative/nonnumeric/nonfinite
amounts, date formatting, month/day boundaries, and Gregorian leap years.
Savings checks cover independent goals, duplicate/missing IDs, invalid names,
amounts and dates, editing, history preservation, deletion, overfunding, exact
cent completion, excessive amounts, total overflow, and ID exhaustion.
Integration checks confirm expense summaries change from $25 to $80 after an
edit and back to $0 after deletion; income and savings allocations do not add
expenses to a $100 food budget.

Storage and backup handoff for Sam
Public accessors expose id, name, target, deadline, and contributions for saving.
To restore one record: validate its saved field types; call create_goal with its
ID/name/target/deadline, add_goal to reject duplicate IDs, and replay each stored
contribution through contribute. Return errors for bad records; do not silently
skip corrupted contributions. Save each user's goal list separately and do not
reuse another user's list. Storage must preserve cents, IDs, order, optional
(deadline None) values, and contribution history. Proposed round-trip checks:
empty list, multiple goals, overfunded goal, edited/cleared deadline, fractional
dollar values, duplicate IDs, malformed fields, unreadable files, and isolation
between two users. These storage checks are planned, not completed: no storage
implementation exists in this checkout. Sam should review savings.mli and work
through test_mina.ml before connecting persistence.
